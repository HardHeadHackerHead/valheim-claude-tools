1.3.0: Claude can be your server's admin. Turn on AllowAdmin (section Requests) when you host or are on the admin list, and ask: `players` lists everyone on the server (not only those near you), `bring`, `goto` and `tp` move players anywhere on the map (landing on ground somebody stands on, saying whether they arrived, and `tp <who> back` to undo), `heal`, `effect`, `tell` and `announce` look after them, `worldtime` sets the world's clock for everyone (only forward), and `save`, `kick`, `ban` and `unban` run the server. The other players need no mod: these are calls the game itself accepts. A player Claude acts on sees who did it, and every action is written to BepInEx/claude/admin.log. `status` now lists everyone connected too (it only listed players near you).

Clawd, a little Claude: type `claude clawd` in the console (F5) and a small orange Claude follows you about, saying in a speech bubble what Claude is doing (each command it runs, and how it went) and whatever Claude wants to tell you (`clawd say ...`). He has a life of his own: he wanders about, goes to look at fires, dropped items, berries, chests, workbenches and tame animals, sits by the fire at night, hides behind you when enemies come, and only now and then stops to look at you. Wear Wrong Universe's jetpack and he puts on a little one too and flies beside you, flames and all. Web-sling with Quad's Webslinger and he swings beside you on a web of his own, zips when you zip and backflips when you flip. New `land <x> <z>`: the biome and ground height anywhere in the world, loaded or not (is a teleport target in the freezing Mountains?). He has moods (happy, sad, thinking, waving, dancing, scared, love, asleep), remarks on night, rain, a new land and being hurt (`claude clawd chatty off` quietens him), naps when you stand still, answers when his name is said in chat, and likes being petted (use key). Only you see him; `claude clawd bye` sends him away.

Reloads show in the game's chat. When Claude (`reload <mod>`) or AutoReload reloads a mod, the chat says "[Claude Tools] Reloaded Parkour 0.1.3" (with "forced" and why when it was), or which mod couldn't start again and why; a rebuilt mod that has to wait for the main menu or a restart says so there too, and Claude Tools reloading itself says its new version (or that the old copy is back).

1.2.15: New `tcdoctor`: the ground's change records. `report` finds a spot with two records and a record that never attached to its ground (made before the ground loaded; the work there looks undone, and a friend joining can loop on the loading screen), `reattach` makes such records again with their ground (nothing saved changes), and `remove` takes a record away on the host; `points`, `tilled` and `untill` look at and undo cultivating in a box. Built from the tool that fixed a stuck join on 2026-10-12.

1.2.14: Stutter. modcheck now follows Update into the methods it calls, so a scene search on a timer in a helper is found (and FindObjectsByType, the newer name, counts too); lookups done once are left alone, and searches deep down (often a key or a command) are notes. A new rule, world-zdo-scan, finds searches of every saved object in the world reachable from Update (a warning when run to the end in one frame). New template, templates/Registry.cs: Live<T> (your own list of a component instead of searching the scene) and Sliced (a big job a slice per frame, resting between passes). A new pitfall, "Big jobs on a timer stutter", with the real costs found tonight, in the docs and the pitfalls skill; the pre-release skill has a step to measure with lag. `lag` can look closer: `mod=<Name>` times every method of a mod (which part of its Update is slow) and `methods=Type.Method` adds game methods such as ZNetScene.CreateObjects.

1.2.13: New: `lag [seconds]` finds what makes the game stutter. For that long it times every mod's Update methods, coroutines and patches, and the game's own Updates, then reports each slow frame (what ran in it, by mod, memory cleanups, log lines) and how often they come, plus mods whose old hot-reload copy still runs; everything it adds comes off again at the end. The valheim-fix-errors skill says how to use it. Its first use found a BuildOrders check taking 300 ms every 5 seconds and a QualityOfLife scan taking 40 ms every second. Also new: `raven` (what Hugin and Munin have to say, mark it seen, or `raven dismiss` to send them away and empty their queue, which the game carries into other worlds) and `logtrace` (stack traces on the game's warnings, to find what keeps logging one).

1.2.12: `console <command>` runs one of the game's own console commands from a request file and gives back what it printed (raiseskill, setkey, setpower, god, spawn...), so testing needs no typing in the game: cheat commands only where cheats are allowed, with devcommands switched on for just that command, and only on a character already marked as cheated (`console confirmcheats`, asked first). New `play [character] [world]` (from the main menu straight into a single-player world, waiting until you're in it), `logout` and `quit` (both save, as the pause menu does). Requests are picked up the moment they're written (the game watches the folder; it still checks every second as a backup), and the next one waiting starts at once. New `clear` (take away trees, rocks, plants, built pieces, items, creatures or all of it around a spot) and `terrain level` / `terrain reset` (flatten the ground around a spot the game's own way, or put it back), for making test sites. `reload ClaudeTools` now reloads Claude Tools itself, no restart needed. New commands for testing without anyone at the keyboard: `words`, `skills`, `playerdata`, `effects`, `fields`, `itemdata`, `inv move`, `craft`; `inventory` shows each item's saved data.

1.2.11: A new pitfall in the skills and docs: scaling a hit with HitData.ApplyModifier leaves its untyped damage (m_damage.m_damage) unscaled; use m_damage.Modify. Found when a boss's toughness let an untyped blow take 48% of its health instead of 8%.

1.2.11: Being found on Thunderstore. modkit package now refuses a package with no category saying what the mod is, and warns when it is neither client-side nor server-side, isn't in the current game update's category, or has fewer than 5 categories (the most-downloaded Valheim mods have about 6); it notes a short description, or one that spends words on "Valheim". The valheim-thunderstore skill has a new step for the description and categories: Thunderstore's search reads only the name and description (parts of words, every word typed; not the README), so the description carries the words players type, other spellings included ("jet ski" and "jetski").

1.2.10: New template ItemReload.cs: a mod that adds items can be reloaded while you play, with no item lost (the items stay registered across the reload, the new copy replaces them by name, and the ones in bags, chests and on the ground get the new copy's prefab). modcheck (and reload) give such a mod the world level when every item it adds goes through ItemReload and it calls Repoint; recipes and status effects still need the main menu. A new pitfall: a container's saved contents are bytes, not a string (reading them as a string makes every chest look empty).

1.2.9: New: daytime <hh:mm | 0-1 | off> holds the game at a time of day (pictures in daylight, as the console's tod does; only where cheats are allowed). Fixed: shoot tp to a spot far away and high above the ground left the player in the air (the game's teleport only checks for a floor somewhere below), and they fell: it now sets them down on the floor with no fall counted, and decides near or far by the distance along the ground. publish.ps1 (and the project template's) reads the plugin's public Name, when a mod has other Name constants.

1.2.8: screen and wait work at the main menu too, so an assistant can start the game for you (Start Game, your character, your world: screen click), and screen now sees windows drawn with the scene's camera (the main menu's: it listed nothing there before). The project template's publish.ps1 builds only the mods you name (-Mods MyMod), leaving the others as they are in dist/.

The look skill says so, and to check every window in Chinese once. The project template has a starter CLAUDE.md and switches the Valheim skills on for itself; docs/getting-started.md is the whole setup.
